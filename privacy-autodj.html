Auto DJ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

No data collection

Auto DJ collects no personal data of any kind. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing leaves your device.

No network access

The app declares no INTERNET permission and never opens a network connection. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

On-device analysis only

Song tempo, key, and energy are computed on your phone and cached on-device only. Deleting the app removes the cache.

Microphone permission

Auto DJ asks for the microphone permission only because Android requires it for the on-device audio visualizer. The Android Visualizer API captures the audio output being played — it is not the microphone. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted. The permission is only requested when you open the visualizer; saying no simply leaves the visuals off.

Crash log

If the app crashes, a stack trace is written to the app's external-files folder (crash.log) so it can be reported with a screenshot. It is never transmitted anywhere.
